# Vehicle 3D models

Used by the live map's 3D vehicle layer (deck.gl `ScenegraphLayer`, see `src/components/map/vehicle3d/`) when the map is tilted and a vehicle is rendered as an individual (not clustered). Flat 2D icons (`VehicleIcon.tsx`) are used everywhere else.

## Sourced (Kenney Car Kit, CC0)

`sedan.glb`, `van.glb`, `truck.glb`, `truck-flat.glb` + `Textures/colormap.png` — from Kenney's Car Kit, mirrored in glTF-binary form at [github.com/shorepine/kenney](https://github.com/shorepine/kenney/tree/main/3d/car). License: **CC0 1.0** (public domain — no attribution required; Kenney asks for a credit as a courtesy, not a condition). Each `.glb` references `Textures/colormap.png` by a relative URI — keep that subfolder alongside the models.

## Procedural (no external source)

`bus.glb`, `semi.glb`, `tanker.glb` — Kenney's Car Kit has no bus, articulated semi-trailer, or tanker-trailer model, and no other CC0 pack with a matching art style and license was found (see `agent/work/decisions.md`, 2026-10-02 "3D vehicle models"). Built from primitives (boxes + cylinders) in the same flat-shaded, toy-block style, fully owned, generated by `scripts/generate-vehicle-models.mjs`. Regenerate with `node scripts/generate-vehicle-models.mjs`.

## Model-space convention

+X = right, +Y = up, +Z = forward (the direction the vehicle drives), origin on the ground under the vehicle's centre.
