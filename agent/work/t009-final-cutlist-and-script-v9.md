# T-009 — v9 cut list and voiceover script (2026-10-14)

**This supersedes `t009-final-cutlist-and-script-v8.md`.** v9 changed no visuals/captions from v8 —
only click SFX — but this file does two things that one didn't:

1. **Timestamps are re-verified against the actual `convoyai_portfolio_cut_v9.mp4`** (5:46.3, not
   5:45.6 — two beats got ~0.3s/~0.35s longer in v9 so a real click would land inside them, which
   pushed everything after them later by about 0.7s). Verified by extracting real frames from v9 at
   each boundary, not just arithmetic.
2. **Every line's word count was checked against its actual time budget** (natural speaking pace is
   roughly 2.2-2.8 words/second) and rewritten where it didn't fit — some beats run long on screen
   (14s) but the old line was short enough to finish in 4s, leaving dead air; a couple of quick cuts
   (2.5s) had lines that would've required rushed, unnatural speech to fit. **Every line below is
   sized to actually fill its timestamp range at a normal conversational pace** — that's the main
   point of this rewrite, not just relabeling v8's numbers.

**Record against THIS file, not v8's.**

**How to record:** read at a normal, relaxed conversational pace — don't force exact timing to the
second. The ranges are a pacing guide (how much room you have for each line), not a metronome. A few
beats are intentionally short/silent (noted below) — don't force a line into those. Once you hand me
the recording, I'll sync it to picture; it doesn't need to be frame-exact on your end.
